HenWen's frontend hardcodes root-absolute URLs in about 300 places -- 274 in inline JS ("/api/...", "/static/...", "/login") plus 27 HTML attributes. That is why the app could not be served under a URL path prefix. Editing 300 call sites would be a huge, risky diff for a deployment option, so the JS half is handled here instead: one adapter that prefixes root-absolute app URLs as they are used.
